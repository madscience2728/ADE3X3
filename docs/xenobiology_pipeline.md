*Proof:* If A = u⊗v and B = w⊗x (outer products, i.e., rank-1), then AB = u(v·w)⊗x = (v·w)(u⊗x). The Keth-Varai prove this by constructing the physical wavefront: propagating a pure-correlation pattern through another pure-correlation pattern yields a pure-correlation pattern, scaled by the "resonance" between them. ∎

**Corollary:** The Lek operation PRESERVES the Rek structure. This means the Keth-Varai can reason about Lek entirely in terms of Rek. They never need to think about 9 independent entries being combined — they think about rank-1 terms being composed.

### 3.6 — Why 27 Never Appears

In human mathematics, the "naive" algorithm computes each of the 9 entries of C = AB as a dot product of length 3:

$$C_{ij} = \sum_{k=1}^{3} A_{ik} B_{kj}$$

This requires 9 × 3 = 27 scalar multiplications. The number 27 arises because we:
1. Decompose the output into 9 independent scalars
2. Compute each scalar as a sum of 3 products
3. Count: 9 × 3 = 27

The Keth-Varai never do step 1. They don't decompose the output into entries. They decompose it into RANK-1 TERMS. The output matrix C has rank at most 3 (it lives in 3×3 space), so:

$$C = \text{(at most 3 Rek)}$$

The question is not "how many scalar mults to compute 9 entries" but "how many Rek in the Lek-pattern's tensor."

**The 27 requires entry-wise thinking. The Keth-Varai think Rek-wise.** They never decompose a matrix into its 9 scalar entries because that decomposition is UNNATURAL to them — it requires choosing a basis, which is an arbitrary coordinate choice, which the Keth-Varai abhor (their world has no preferred coordinates — three magnetic axes, none primary).

### 4.4 — The Communication Problem (ARC β CONTINUATION)

Two Keth-Varai want to transmit the fact "I computed A·B and got C" across a distance, using their magnetic field modulation. The communication cost is proportional to the number of Rek terms transmitted:

- Transmit A: 3 Rek terms (rank-1 decomposition of a generic 3×3 matrix)

The Keth-Varai formulate tensor rank as a COMMUNICATION COMPLEXITY problem:

$$\text{Rek-Vot}(T_{\text{lek}}) = \text{minimum Rek per Lek in the optimal protocol}$$

This is their version of the tensor rank question, and it maps exactly onto the human formulation — but motivated by communication (survival: predator alerts, mating signals, colony coordination) rather than by computation.
